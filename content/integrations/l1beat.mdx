## Overview

L1Beat tracks Avalanche and every L1 on it: activity, validators, fees, interchain messages and money moving in and out of Avalanche. Everything on the site is also available through a free public API.

## Features

- **L1s and validators**: every Avalanche L1 with its validators, fees paid to the P-Chain, and a risk and decentralization profile for each chain.
- **Network metrics**: activity, fees and growth across L1s, with side-by-side comparisons of up to four chains.
- **Interchain**: ICM messages between L1s and the C-Chain, the apps sending them, and the value they move.
- **Bridges**: value bridged into and out of Avalanche, by bridge, chain and token.
- **Markets**: DEX volume and top pools, GMX perpetuals, Aave and BENQI lending, and stablecoins on the C-Chain.
- **AVAX**: staking and liquid staking, AVAX burned, and AVAX held by public companies and spot ETFs.
- **ACPs**: every Avalanche Community Proposal, searchable.

## Getting Started

1. Browse any L1 at [l1beat.io](https://l1beat.io/).
2. Try the API in the [playground](https://l1beat.io/api), or call it directly:

```bash
curl "https://api.l1beat.io/api/v1/data/chains?active=true&limit=5"
```

## Use Cases

- **L1 teams**: track your chain's validators, fees and interchain activity, and compare it with other L1s.
- **Builders**: pull Avalanche data into apps, bots and dashboards without running your own infrastructure.
- **Researchers and analysts**: follow staking, DeFi, bridge flows and AVAX treasuries over time.

## Documentation

- 50+ REST endpoints plus a live block WebSocket, at `https://api.l1beat.io`.
- No API key, open CORS, 180 requests a minute per IP.
- Try every endpoint in the browser at [l1beat.io/api](https://l1beat.io/api), with curl, fetch and Python snippets.
- OpenAPI 3 spec: [api.l1beat.io/openapi.json](https://api.l1beat.io/openapi.json).
- EVM, DeFi and interchain data cover the C-Chain; P-Chain, validator and fee data cover every L1.
