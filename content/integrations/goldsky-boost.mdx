## Overview

Goldsky Boost is a free JSON-RPC cache for Avalanche C-Chain. It serves eligible historical reads from indexed data and forwards other requests to your existing provider.

## Features

- **Historical reads**: Caches block, transaction, receipt, and fixed-range log queries.
- **Provider continuity**: Forwards writes, state reads, traces, and latest-block queries to your provider.
- **Free usage**: No charge for cached or forwarded requests, subject to fair use.

## Getting Started

Configure your existing HTTPS RPC provider, then point JSON-RPC requests to `https://edge.goldsky.com/boost/avalanche?key=YOUR_API_KEY`. Keep WebSocket subscriptions on your provider.
