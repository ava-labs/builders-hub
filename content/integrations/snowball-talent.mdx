## Overview

Snowball Talent is a talent support service from Ava Labs for teams building on Avalanche. It gives ecosystem partners access to Ava Labs' internal recruiting infrastructure to hire, scale, and grow their teams. It is aimed at early-stage teams (pre-seed to Series A) and partners facing hard-to-fill technical roles in blockchain, infrastructure, and protocol engineering.

## Services

- **Snowball Talent Enablement**: A retained advisory service covering recruitment process design, sourcing strategy, headcount planning, and access to a talent community.
- **Snowball Recruiting**: A contingency-based service handling full-cycle recruitment for high-priority technical and executive roles, without retainers.

## Pricing

Snowball Recruiting runs on a contingency model: 10% of first-year base salary per placement, capped at a flat $15,000, whichever is lower. There are no retainers or stacked fees, and no cost if a placement is not made.

## Getting Started

1. Visit the [Snowball Talent site](https://snowball-talent.netlify.app/) to review the services.
2. Share the roles you are hiring for, or the talent challenge you want help with.
3. Engage through Enablement for advisory support or Recruiting for full-cycle hiring.

## Use Cases

- **Early-stage hiring**: Pre-seed to Series A teams making their first technical hires.
- **Hard-to-fill roles**: Sourcing for specialized blockchain, infrastructure, and protocol engineering positions.
- **Recruitment maturity**: Advisory for teams standing up their own hiring processes and sourcing strategy.
